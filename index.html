About Me

For several years, I managed a small business, where I was responsible for bookkeeping, inventory, marketing, and logistics. This experience taught me how closely everyday business decisions depend on accurate information, careful organization, and an understanding of both numbers and people.

My education in applied mathematics strengthened my quantitative reasoning and problem-solving skills, while my studies in economics gave me a broader understanding of business and economic decision-making. Together, these experiences shape how I approach analytical work: I look beyond the calculations to understand the practical question behind the data.

I am seeking opportunities where I can apply my quantitative, analytical, and business background to solve practical problems and support informed decision-making. I value work that requires curiosity, precision, continuous learning, and a thoughtful approach to complex questions.
